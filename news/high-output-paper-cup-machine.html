High Output Paper Cup Machine

Published 2026-11-21 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

A high output paper cup machine for paper cup and bowl producers runs at 3245–4745 cups per minute, and that figure only means something alongside the wall structure and the format range. This guide covers both, plus the maintenance routine that keeps the output consistent.

What This Equipment Does

Capacity in cups per minute is only one number in the decision. The high output paper cup machine also has to seal 20 L format, hold the 3.0 ml tolerance and warm up in a time the shift can absorb.

Key Technical Specifications

For a paper cup and bowl producers buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: 316L stainless steel product-contact parts, 7-inch colour touch controls, 3.0 ml tolerance, 15-minute changeover, 5.5–7.5 kW connected load and a 1800 × 2200 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A high output paper cup machine becomes part of the paper cup and bowl producers production line when the same recipe reaches it, the same cleaning reaches it and the same fault code reaches the operator. This build is specified around those three handovers.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the high output paper cup machine on the worst week of the year. A line running 20 L format with a 15-minute changeover either survives the peak or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

For a paper cup and bowl producers operation the practical measure of after-sales support is how quickly the high output paper cup machine returns to 3245 cups per minute after a fault, and that is decided by parts availability rather than by the size of the service team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

What spare parts should be held for the high output paper cup machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 3245 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Can the high output paper cup machine be upgraded later?

Within the 3245–4745 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 20 L range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
