Can the disposable salad bowl useful run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The paper cup and bowl producers team still owns the routine checks, but the 10-minute changeover is guided.

How does the disposable salad bowl useful behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the disposable salad bowl useful determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.
