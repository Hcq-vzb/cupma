Soup Paper Cup Machine

Published 2026-11-16 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

Most disagreements about a soup paper cup machine come down to what was never written down. This guide sets out the paper cup and bowl producers requirements that decide whether a machine works: what it must deliver at 16607–21107 units per hour, what it is built from, and what to verify before handover.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a soup paper cup machine is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 16607 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the paper cup and bowl producers production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask what a supplier is not willing to write down. The answer, on a soup paper cup machine, is usually the tolerance at the low end of the range: this build holds 0.5 ml at 16607 units per hour, not only at 21107.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream blank feed is where a cup forming line usually fails. Size the magazine for the 16607 cups per minute duty so the soup paper cup machine is not waiting on the paper supplier.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the soup paper cup machine over five years rather than one budget cycle. Mould sets, contact parts in 304 stainless steel and the 0.5 ml tolerance all have different cost curves over that horizon.

Maintenance and Service

Consumable life on a soup paper cup machine depends on the paper, not only on the cycle count. Track how many 500 ml cups each mould set produces, because the number will differ between paper suppliers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Does the soup paper cup machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 1400 × 3200 mm GA drawing.

How is the soup paper cup machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
