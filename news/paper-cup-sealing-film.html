Paper Cup Sealing Film

Published 2026-11-28 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

Where a paper cup sealing film specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the paper cup and bowl producers requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

Because a paper cup sealing film handles a defined step for paper cup and bowl producers, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 1 L product at 7182–10182 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This paper cup sealing film draws 6.5–8.5 kW across a 2400 × 3600 × 2400 mm footprint with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This paper cup sealing film takes a standard encoder signal and a 2400 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the paper cup sealing film to real 1 L volume, then let the 2.0 ml tolerance and the 7182–10182 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a paper cup sealing film with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and 7182–10182 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the paper cup sealing film be upgraded later?

Within the 7182–10182 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 1 L range before ordering.

How long does a format changeover take on this paper cup sealing film?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 20 minutes. The first change to a new 1 L format takes longer.

Can the paper cup sealing film run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The paper cup and bowl producers team still owns the routine checks, but the 20-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
