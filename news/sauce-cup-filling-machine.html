Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this sauce cup filling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±2.0 ml on 250–500 ml containers at 8030–11030 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

What spare parts should be held for the sauce cup filling machine?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 8030 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

Can the sauce cup filling machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 8030–11030 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 250–500 ml range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
