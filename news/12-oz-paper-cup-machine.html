The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For a 12 oz paper cup machine in paper cup and bowl producers production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

Think of a 12 oz paper cup machine as the piece of equipment that determines the ceiling on the whole paper cup and bowl producers production line. It forms 500 ml format at 9140–12640 cups per minute, and nothing downstream can outrun it.

Key Technical Specifications

For a 12 oz paper cup machine aimed at paper cup and bowl producers operations, the practical reference build is 316L stainless steel contact parts, a 4-inch HMI and a 1.5–2.5 kW connected load inside 2400 × 2200 × 2100 mm.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the 12 oz paper cup machine determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 9140–12640 units per hour and a 8-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure. Size the 12 oz paper cup machine to real 500 ml volume and let the 0.3 ml tolerance and the 9140–12640 cups per minute figure earn their keep on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive work on the 12 oz paper cup machine is concentrated on the wear parts, and the recommended interval is derived from the 9140–12640 duty range rather than from a generic calendar figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the 12 oz paper cup machine?

The reference build draws 1.5–2.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How long does a format changeover take on this 12 oz paper cup machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 500 ml format takes longer.

What accuracy can this 12 oz paper cup machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.3 ml on 500 ml product at 9140–12640 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
