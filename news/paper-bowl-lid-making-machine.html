Paper Bowl Lid Making Machine

Published 2026-11-05 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

A paper bowl lid making machine for paper cup and bowl producers is usually bought to remove a manual step. This guide sets out what the machine must deliver at 15204–19704 units per hour, how it interfaces with the line, and the checks to complete before handover.

What This Equipment Does

Position a paper bowl lid making machine by what it removes from the paper cup and bowl producers process. This configuration handles 330 ml can product at 15204–19704 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a paper bowl lid making machine on rated capacity alone. A 15204–19704 units per hour machine with a 1.5 ml tolerance and food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating a paper bowl lid making machine as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 1600 mm working width, it becomes part of the paper cup and bowl producers control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a paper bowl lid making machine is downtime avoided during a 20-minute format change on a 330 ml can format, because the paper cup and bowl producers production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

Choose a supplier that holds spare parts locally and answers support requests in writing. Preventive work on the paper bowl lid making machine concentrates on the seals, sensors and drives — wear items rather than failure items.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the paper bowl lid making machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Can the paper bowl lid making machine run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 4-inch HMI, yes. The paper cup and bowl producers team still owns the routine checks, but the 20-minute changeover is guided.

How does the paper bowl lid making machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the paper bowl lid making machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
