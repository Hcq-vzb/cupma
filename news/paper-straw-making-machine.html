Frequently Asked Questions

Can the paper straw making machine handle a 12 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 20-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

Can the paper straw making machine be upgraded later?

Within the 14996–18996 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 12 oz range before ordering.

How long does a format changeover take on this paper straw making machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 20 minutes. The first change to a new 12 oz format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
