Frequently Asked Questions

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for paper cup and bowl producers operations running audits.

What is the delivery lead time for the double wall paper cup machine?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 330 ml set is included.

Is the double wall paper cup machine suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 7707–10707 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
