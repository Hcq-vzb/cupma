A cup lid forming machine for paper cup and bowl producers runs at 5875–7375 cups per minute, and that figure only means something alongside the wall structure and the format range. This guide covers both, plus the maintenance routine that keeps the output consistent.

What This Equipment Does

Every seam on a paper cup originates at the forming station. This cup lid forming machine holds the 1.0 ml bottom tolerance while forming 330 ml can format at 5875–7375 cups per minute.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare a cup lid forming machine on cavities and on the material it is designed for, not just on cups per minute. This build handles 330 ml can format at 5875–7375 cups per minute using SUS304 with sanitary fittings on the product-contact surfaces.

Integration With Your Line

Shared CIP and control logic are worth agreeing early. A cup lid forming machine that speaks the same protocol as the rest of the paper cup and bowl producers production line can be called up in a recipe change instead of being restarted by hand.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Treat the 10-minute format change as a priced item. On the paper cup and bowl producers production line that switches 330 ml can format often, a fast mould change is output rather than convenience.

Maintenance and Service

Availability planning for a cup lid forming machine starts with the assumption that a seal will need replacing in the next quarter. Holding the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact-part spares on site is what keeps a 10-minute repair a 10-minute repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the cup lid forming machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 2800 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

How does the cup lid forming machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the cup lid forming machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
