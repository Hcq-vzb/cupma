Paper Bucket Making Machine

Published 2026-11-25 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

Specifying a paper bucket making machine for paper cup and bowl producers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

Ask a paper cup and bowl producers plant engineer to describe their best-performing paper bucket making machine and they will describe the one that runs for weeks without intervention. This configuration is specified at 7786–10786 units per hour on 1 L product with that as the design target.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two paper bucket making machine quotations look identical, the difference is in the contact parts and the control platform. This reference build specifies SUS304 with sanitary fittings surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 0.5 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover across 1 L format.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This paper bucket making machine already handles a standard encoder interface and a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the paper bucket making machine over five years rather than one budget cycle. SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts, a 0.5 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

A paper bucket making machine that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the paper bucket making machine suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 7786–10786 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Can the paper bucket making machine run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The paper cup and bowl producers team still owns the routine checks, but the 8-minute changeover is guided.

What is the actual connected load of the paper bucket making machine?

The reference build draws 3–4 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
