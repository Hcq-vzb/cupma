Double PE Coated Cup Machine

Published 2026-11-17 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

Choosing the right double PE coated cup machine is a practical decision for paper cup and bowl producers. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting a double PE coated cup machine on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a double PE coated cup machine is not the demonstration but the third month. At 9086–12586 units per hour for 8 oz product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that limits output on a double PE coated cup machine is usually changeover. At 15 minutes per 8 oz format and 9086–12586 units per hour, campaigns have to be planned around the stop.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream of a double PE coated cup machine the cups still have to be counted, stacked and packed, and the paper cup and bowl producers production line real capacity is limited by whichever of those steps is slowest.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include mould sets and spares in the numbers. A double PE coated cup machine with a 10-inch colour touch and a 15-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a paper cup and bowl producers buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Train the people who run the shift, not only the commissioning engineer. On this double PE coated cup machine the wear items are simple to identify — seals, drive, guide rails — and most paper cup and bowl producers downtime is caused by a part that was available but not swapped.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for paper cup and bowl producers operations running audits.

Can the double PE coated cup machine handle a 8 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 15-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

What is the delivery lead time for the double PE coated cup machine?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 8 oz set is included.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
