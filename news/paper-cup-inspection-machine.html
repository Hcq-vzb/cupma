Frequently Asked Questions

Is a food-grade 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 20 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What accuracy can this paper cup inspection machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.3 ml on 20 L product at 6182–7682 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for paper cup and bowl producers operations running audits.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
