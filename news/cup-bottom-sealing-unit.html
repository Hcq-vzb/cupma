Cup Bottom Sealing Unit

Published 2026-10-28 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying a cup bottom sealing unit. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the paper cup and bowl producers production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

A cup bottom sealing unit earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 3793–5293 units per hour for 1.5 L product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference cup bottom sealing unit uses 304/316L stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 4-inch HMI, a 36–51 kW connected load and a 2400 × 2200 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating a cup bottom sealing unit as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 2400 mm working width, it becomes part of the paper cup and bowl producers control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the cup bottom sealing unit against the cost of the manual step it replaces. At 3793 units per hour the labour difference over a year is usually the largest number in the case.

Maintenance and Service

Keep the sensor set and the drive belt on site for the cup bottom sealing unit, so a fault is a 10-minute repair rather than a week of waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the cup bottom sealing unit need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 2200 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

How does the cup bottom sealing unit behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the cup bottom sealing unit determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
