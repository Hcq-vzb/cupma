Paper Cup Packaging Machine

Published 2026-11-20 · Jiangsu KIWL Machinery

A paper cup packaging machine sits on the critical path of the paper cup and bowl producers production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a paper cup packaging machine.

What This Equipment Does

For a paper cup and bowl producers operation, the paper cup packaging machine is where a production target either becomes repeatable or stays theoretical. This unit runs at 14501–18501 units per hour on 16 oz product with a measured deviation rather than a quoted one.

Key Technical Specifications

A paper cup packaging machine is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 14501–18501 units per hour, contact parts 316L stainless steel, control 10-inch colour touch, load 19–26 kW, footprint 3200 × 1400 × 2800 mm, changeover 8 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 3200 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a paper cup packaging machine arrives on a paper cup and bowl producers floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a paper cup packaging machine is arithmetic: every unit of 1.0 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 8-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the paper cup and bowl producers production line.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a paper cup packaging machine with 316L stainless steel contact parts and 14501–18501 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the paper cup packaging machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the paper cup packaging machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Does the paper cup packaging machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 1400 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
