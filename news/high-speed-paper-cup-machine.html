Frequently Asked Questions

Can the high speed paper cup machine handle a 2 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 12-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How does the high speed paper cup machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the high speed paper cup machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

Can the high speed paper cup machine run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The paper cup and bowl producers team still owns the routine checks, but the 12-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
